# Consumer health data privacy policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This page is for people who live in Washington State or Nevada, and for people whose health data is collected there. We follow it for all our users.

It is about "consumer health data". That means information that is linked to you, or could reasonably be linked to you, and that says something about your past, present or future health. Our general privacy policy is at [PRIVACY POLICY URL].

[APP NAME] is made by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], a small independent company registered in Hong Kong.

## What we do not collect

Your records in [APP NAME] are stored on your phone. This includes your medication, doses, weight, side effects and questions for your doctor. We do not get a copy. The only items the app sends us are the usage counts listed below, and only with your consent.

## Consumer health data we collect, and why

We collect consumer health data in two cases only.

### 1. Usage counts, only with your consent

The app asks for your consent before it sends anything. If you consent, we collect:

- a random install ID and the version of the app
- with each count, the number of days since you finished setup (a number, not a date)
- that you finished setup, with your three setup answers: how long you have taken your medication, what you used before to keep track, and where you heard about us
- whether you take an injection or a pill
- whether you logged a dose: "activated" (soon after setup), this week (yes or no, with the number of the week), "week-2 active", and "four-week streak met"
- that you marked your medication as paused or stopped
- that you tapped "I would want this" under "Want Pro?"

Our server also stores the date each count arrived. It does not store the time of day.

We treat all of this as consumer health data. It shows that the person behind the random ID uses an app for GLP-1 medication.

The counts do not include the name of your medication, your dose, your weight, your symptoms or anything you type.

**Why we collect it and how we use it.** We count how many people finish setup, keep logging, pause or stop, and would want a paid plan. We use the totals to decide what to fix and whether to keep building the app. We may publish totals. We do not use this data for advertising. We do not use it to build a profile of you or to make decisions about you.

### 2. Health details in email you send us

If you email us and write about your health, we receive what you wrote. We use it only to answer you and to handle your request.

## Where the data comes from

From you. It comes from the app on your phone, after you consent, and from email you choose to send us.

## Your rights and how to use them

- **Confirm.** You can ask whether we are collecting, sharing or selling your consumer health data.
- **Access.** You can ask for a copy of it. You can also ask for a list of all third parties and affiliates we shared it with or sold it to, with a way to contact each one. Today that list is empty.
- **Withdraw consent.** You can turn usage counts off at any time in [SETTINGS PATH].
- **Delete.** When you turn usage counts off, or delete all data in the app, the counts tied to your install ID are deleted from our server. You can also ask us by email. We will delete the data from our records and tell our processors to do the same. Backup copies are removed within [BACKUP RETENTION PERIOD].

**How to ask.** Email [CONTACT EMAIL]. For usage counts, include your install ID. The app shows it in [SETTINGS PATH] while usage counts are on. We need it because we do not know your name. Requests are free. We will answer within 45 days. If we need more time, we will tell you why, and we will take no more than 45 more days.

**If we say no.** You can appeal by replying to our answer within [APPEAL WINDOW]. We will answer in writing within 45 days and explain our reasons. If we turn down your appeal, you can complain to the Washington State Attorney General at www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint [TO CONFIRM: link] or to the Nevada Attorney General [LAWYER: add link].

We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Before we collect or share a new kind of consumer health data, or use it for a new purpose, we will update this page and ask for your consent. The date at the top shows when this page last changed.
