# Pricing and promise

Last updated: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

[APP NAME] is a logging tool for people who take a GLP-1 medication. This page says what is free, how we will charge if we sell a paid plan later, and what happens to your records. A change log is at the bottom. When we change this page, we write down what changed and why.

## Who we are

[APP NAME] is made by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME]. We are a small independent company registered in Hong Kong. We are not connected to any company that makes medications.

## Promise 1: this list stays free

These features are free. They will stay free for as long as we run [APP NAME].

- Logging shots and pills
- The estimated medication level chart (for some brand-name weekly shots only; the numbers come from US prescribing information)
- Medication reminders
- Weight and side effect logging
- Danger-symptom alerts
- The list of questions for your doctor
- The weekly summary (numbers only)
- Export of all your records
- Restore from your own export file

How the list works:

- Every feature we release before the paid plan goes on sale is added to this list on the day we release it.
- Only features we release on or after that day may cost money.
- We will not take a feature off this list, and we will not move it behind a payment.
- If a law or an app store review requires us to change or remove a feature on this list, we will do it. We will write the reason in the change log.

## Promise 2: fair billing

There is no paid plan today. Everything in the app is free.

We plan to sell a paid plan called Pro later. The planned price is $29.99 a year. We may also offer a monthly plan; the planned price for that is $4.99 a month. If the plan changes before Pro goes on sale, we will say so in the change log.

When Pro goes on sale, these rules apply:

- No free trial that turns into a charge on its own.
- At any one time, in the same app store, every new subscriber to the same plan pays the same price. A yearly plan and a monthly plan are two plans, each with its own price. The only exception is a founder price for a limited number of places. We will post the number of places on this page before it starts.
- As long as your subscription does not lapse, your renewal price will not be higher than the price you first paid.
- The app will remind you 14 days before a yearly plan renews. This is on top of anything the app store sends you.

Under "Want Pro?" the app has a button that says "I would want this". Tapping it does not charge you and does not unlock anything. It only tells us that someone is interested, and only if you agreed to usage counts.

## Promise 3: your data is yours

- You can export all your records at any time. You do not need to sign up.
- If we ever stop running [APP NAME], we will give 90 days' notice first. Export will keep working during those 90 days.

We do not have your email address, so we cannot write to you. We will post the notice in the app, on this page and in the app store listing.

## If a law or an app store requires a change

We follow the law and the rules of the app stores. If a law, a regulator or an app store review requires us to change or remove something on this page, we will do what is required. We will write what changed and why in the change log.
