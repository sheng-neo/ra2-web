## What stays on your phone

The app stores your records on your phone. Your records are:

- your medication, dose and schedule
- each shot or pill you log
- your weight and the side effects you log
- your answers to the setup questions, including the safety questions
- the country or region you choose at setup
- questions you save for your doctor
- your settings

We do not get a copy of these records. We cannot see them. We cannot get them back for you if they are lost.

There is one exception. If you agree to usage counts, the app sends us the short list of items under "What we receive". The list includes three of your setup answers, whether you take an injection or a pill, whether you logged a dose in a given week, and the fact that you marked your medication as paused or stopped. Nothing outside that list is sent.

Because your records are on your phone:

- If you delete the app or lose your phone, your records are gone unless you exported a copy first.
- When you export, the app makes a file on your phone. You decide where that file goes. If you put it in an email, a message or a cloud drive, that service's own rules apply to it. [TO CONFIRM: the export file is not encrypted.]

## What we receive

### 1. Usage counts, only if you agree

The app asks if it may send us usage counts. It asks once, on the last screen of setup, and you can change your answer at any time in [SETTINGS PATH]. If you say no, nothing is sent, and every feature works the same. [TO CONFIRM: that this wording matches the screen of the store build.]

If you say yes, the app sends these, and only these:

| What is sent | When |
|---|---|
| A random install ID. The app makes it up. It is not your phone's own ID. It is not made from your name or your app store account. | With each count |
| The version of the app | With each count |
| The number of days since you finished setup. This is a number, not a date. | With each count |
| "Setup finished", with your three setup answers (how long you have taken your medication, what you used before to keep track, and where you heard about us) and whether you take an injection or a pill | Once |
| "Activated": you logged a dose soon after setup | Once |
| Whether you logged a dose this week: yes or no, with the number of the week (week 1, week 2 and so on, counted from your first logged dose). A dose counts for the week you entered it in the app, not the week you say you took it. | Each week |
| "Week-2 active": you were still logging in your second week | Once |
| "Four-week streak met": you logged most of your planned doses in the four weeks after your first logged dose. Your phone works this out. We get only the result. If you turn usage counts on after those four weeks, your phone works it out once, at that moment, from the doses already logged and your schedule as it is then. | Once |
| You marked your medication as paused or stopped | When you do it |
| You tapped "I would want this" under "Want Pro?" | When you tap it |

The counts do not include the name of your medication, your dose, your weight, your symptoms or anything you type.

We treat these counts as health information. They show that the person behind the ID uses an app for GLP-1 medication.

Our server stores one more thing with each count: the date the count arrived. It does not store the time of day. Counts are sent soon after they happen, so the arrival date, together with the day number, shows the date you finished setup and the date of each thing counted.

When your phone sends a count, our server can see the internet address (IP address) it came from. This is true of any app that talks to a server. Our count server does not store the IP address with the counts. [TO CONFIRM: say here whether the hosting company, or anything that sits in front of the server, logs IP addresses, and for how long.]

## Your choices and rights

You have these choices no matter where you live.

**Your records on your phone**

- See them and change them in the app.
- Export all of them under Me, in "Backup and export". You do not need to sign up.
- Delete all of them under Me, in "Delete all data". If you agreed to usage counts, this also tells our server to delete the counts tied to your install ID.

**Usage counts**

- Turn them on or off at any time in [SETTINGS PATH]. Turning them off deletes the counts tied to your install ID. Every feature works the same with usage counts off.
- Ask us whether we hold counts tied to your install ID, and ask for a copy. Email us the install ID. The app shows it in [SETTINGS PATH] while usage counts are on.

**Email you sent us**

- Ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Please write from the same email address.

**How to ask**

Email [CONTACT EMAIL]. You do not have to say why. Requests are free. We will answer within 45 days. If we need more time, we will tell you why, and we will take no more than 45 more days.

We do not know who you are, so we cannot look you up by name. For usage counts we need your install ID. For email we check that the request comes from the same address.

Someone you trust can ask for you. We will check with you that they have your permission.

**If we say no**

We will tell you why. You can appeal by replying to our answer. We will answer an appeal in writing within 45 days. If you are still not satisfied, you can contact the attorney general of your state.

**No penalty**

We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights.
