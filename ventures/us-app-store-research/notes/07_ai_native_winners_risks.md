## Q1. Which AI-native consumer apps reached the US charts 2024–2026, and what are their revenues and team sizes?

### Takeaway
Money in consumer AI is highly concentrated: ChatGPT alone earns more than twice all other general assistants combined on mobile, and Kling, Speak, Photoroom, Remini and Cal AI are the clearest vertical revenue winners. The best evidence of a small-team win is Cal AI: about 7 employees, roughly $30–50M annual revenue, sold to the incumbent MyFitnessPal in March 2026. Most vertical winners have $10M–$100M a year in revenue, and many viral apps (Sora, Lensa, companion apps) lost users quickly after launch.

### Inferences
- 头部效应极强：通用助手（ChatGPT）吃掉 AI 移动端收入的大头。垂直 AI 应用的现实天花板通常是年收入 $10M–$100M，而不是数十亿。对小团队来说，$1M–$10M ARR 是更可预期的成功区间，例如 Umax 和 Plug AI 这一档。
- 小团队已验证的打法有一个共同模式：一个"拍照或说话 → AI 即时给结果"的单点动作，配合高频习惯场景（饮食记录、外貌、约会、语言口语），再加上 influencer/UGC 投放和硬付费墙。Cal AI 被 MyFitnessPal 收购，说明"更便宜的 AI 替代品"的终局之一是被内容型老牌应用收购，而老牌应用贡献的是它的数据资产（食物数据库）。
- 视频生成（Kling、Hailuo）的收入远高于图片换脸类应用，但这一块是模型公司自营的业务，需要自有模型或很大的推理预算，不适合"小团队替代内容型应用"的打法。
- 爆款下载不等于收入：Sora、Lensa 和 Ghibli 风格应用都是"病毒式峰值 → 一到三个月内衰减"。

## Q2. Failure and caution cases: incumbents dying, wrappers absorbed, hardware/novelty flops, App Review risk, AI-app churn

### Takeaway
The evidence cuts both ways. Incumbents built on human-made content can collapse: Chegg's revenue roughly halved again in 2026, after a 2025 when subscribers fell 31–40%. AI-native apps are also fragile. They churn about 30% faster than non-AI apps, have higher refund rates, and see viral spikes fade within weeks. Regulated categories (Woebot) and the "general assistant adds the feature" risk (Ghibli, Nano Banana) are recurring causes of death.

### Cited Findings

- In May 2025, Chegg cut about 22% of staff (about 248 people) and closed its US and Canada offices. The charges were $34–38M, with savings of $45–55M in 2025 and $100–110M in 2026.
- Chegg blamed Google AI Overviews and students switching to ChatGPT. It also said OpenAI and Anthropic were giving students free access.

## Q3. Unit economics: inference cost trends, gross margins, CAC/UA costs

### Takeaway
At a fixed capability level, per-token LLM prices are falling about 5–13x a year (Epoch, MIT). Image costs about $0.01–$0.17 per image, and video about $0.05–$0.60 per second. That makes text and image "content replacement" cheap to serve. Video is still costly, and heavy users on flat subscriptions can still eat the margin. AI apps run about 45–60% gross margin versus 80–90% for classic software. US paid acquisition is expensive: Apple Ads median CPI is about $4, and Meta CPI was volatile in 2025, peaking around $24.

### Cited Findings

**LLM token price trends**
- Epoch AI ("The Plunging Price of Thought", Sept 2026): at a fixed performance level, prices fall about 47% per quarter, which is about 13x per year. About two years after a model is state of the art, the decline slows to about 32% per quarter, about 4.7x per year. — [Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/publications/the-plunging-price-of-thought); [Pasquale Pillitteri summary](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/21044/epoch-ai-ai-prices-fall-47-percent-quarter)

## Q4. Platform risk: what general assistants now do natively, and which categories keep needing a vertical app

### Takeaway
General assistants have already absorbed homework help and Q&A tutoring (Chegg), one-off image editing and stylization (ChatGPT image generation, Nano Banana), and, via OpenAI's Apps SDK (Oct 2025), are starting to sit in front of travel, real estate, learning and design apps. Proactive daily briefings (ChatGPT Pulse) go after news and summary apps. The categories that survive have a daily habit loop and logging (Cal AI, which was bought by an incumbent for its data), structured progress (Speak at about $100M), regulated or compliance needs, or deep UI and workflow (Photoroom).

- OpenAI launched "apps in ChatGPT" and the Apps SDK on October 6, 2025.
