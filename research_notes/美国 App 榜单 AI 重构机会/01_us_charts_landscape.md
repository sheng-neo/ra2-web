## Q1. Who leads US Top Grossing now (overall and non-game)? Top ~50 non-game grossing apps with category and US revenue

As of Nov 2026, **ChatGPT is the #1 grossing app on the US App Store** (54 consecutive days at #1 as of 2026-11-07), ahead of YouTube and TikTok; in 2025 the US non-game leaders were TikTok, YouTube, Google One, ChatGPT, Tinder, HBO Max, Disney+, with Claude entering the US top 10 in 2026. ## Q2. US consumer spending by App Store category (2024, 2025, 2026) and growth rates

### Takeaway
US non-game app spending was **~$33.6B in 2025 (+26.8% YoY, Appfigures gross)** vs $21.9B for games — non-game is now ~60% of US app spend; total US spend was ~$55–60B (Appfigures/Sensor Tower). Globally, non-game IAP overtook games for the first time in 2025 (Sensor Tower $85.6B vs $81.8B), driven by generative AI, social, video streaming and productivity. A clean **US category-by-category dollar table for 2025 was not retrievable**; only category growth indicators and partial category sizes are available.

### Inferences

## Q5. Subscription economics benchmarks by category (price points, trial conversion, retention)

### Takeaway

- For "AI-produced content" apps, the benchmarks favor **hard paywall + short (≤7-day) trial + weekly plan** in Utilities/Entertainment/Productivity-style apps, but weekly plans retain only ~5.5% at Day 380 — revenue is front-loaded and UA-dependent. H&F/Education, by contrast, monetize on annual plans with better retention.
- The extreme concentration (4.6% of new apps reach $10k MRR; pre-2020 apps hold 69% of revenue) means the opportunity is not "new subscription app" in general but **displacing incumbents whose product is human-made content** with AI-generated equivalents at lower COGS.
- AI apps' +41% revenue per payer / +30% faster churn implies AI-native products command premium pricing but need ongoing novelty (content refresh) — favorable for categories where AI can continuously generate fresh content (stories, drama, coaching plans).

## Q6. How large is the AI-app share of US app spending, and which categories do AI apps land in?

### Takeaway
Globally, gen-AI apps went from ~1% to **~6% of non-game IAP in 2025 (>$5B of $85.6B)** and roughly doubled again YoY in Q2 2026 (+108%); ChatGPT alone is ~60–65% of AI app revenue and became **the #1 grossing app on the US App Store from ~mid-Sep 2026**. AI apps mostly land in **Productivity** (assistants), **Photo & Video** (Sora, Canva AI, CapCut AI), and **Entertainment** (AI companions, a ~$120M/yr niche in 2025). A US-only AI share figure was not published in retrievable sources; derived estimate: **~6–8% of US non-game spend in 2025, likely 10%+ by late 2026**.
