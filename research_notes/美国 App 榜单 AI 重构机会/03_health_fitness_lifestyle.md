> Research date: 2026-11-19. Scope: US iOS App Store, mainly 2025–2026 data. Older data is labeled with its year.

## Q1. Top US apps and revenue/subscriber estimates (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
The highest-grossing US Health & Fitness apps in 2025 were MyFitnessPal, Flo, AllTrails, Strava and Fitbit (Sensor Tower Q2 2025). Spend in the category is growing (Sensor Tower: in-app purchase revenue +13% to $4.5B), while downloads are flat. Several human-labor-heavy incumbents are shrinking or restructuring:
- BetterHelp revenue fell 9%.
- Talkspace consumer revenue fell 30%.
- Peloton paid app subscriptions fell 11%.
- WW went through Chapter 11.
- Noom cut coaches.
- Headspace moved therapists to contract roles.
- Bumble paying users fell about 11.5%, and Match payers fell 5%.

### Inferences
- BetterHelp's implied revenue per paying user: $950M ÷ about 385K average paying users ≈ $2,460 a year ≈ **$205 a month (about $47 a week)**. That is the price ceiling an AI therapy or coaching product undercuts.
- Peloton's app subscriptions fell from 621K to 552K, Talkspace's consumer revenue fell 30%, and BetterHelp's US users fell by a low double-digit rate. Together these suggest that cash-pay demand for human-delivered fitness and therapy is weakening. The sources do not prove AI substitution is the cause; GLP-1 drugs, the post-COVID normalization and payer shifts are confounders.
- MyFitnessPal's weekly US iOS peaks of about $2.8–3M suggest US iOS gross revenue on the order of $100M+ a year. This is a rough extrapolation from weekly peaks, not a reported number.

## Q2. AI-native breakouts: what reached scale and US top grossing?

### Takeaway
**Cal AI** is the clearest AI-native breakout in this space. It took photo-based calorie counting to more than 15M downloads and more than $30M a year in revenue in under two years, bootstrapped, and MyFitnessPal acquired it in March 2026. AI therapy (Ash, $93M raised, free) and AI dating assistants (Rizz, an estimated $150–300K/month) have traction but **are not yet proven top-grossing businesses**. Most of the AI value is being captured by incumbents adding AI layers:
- Peloton IQ
- Strava's acquisitions of Runna and The Breakaway
- Headspace's Ebb
- Bumble's AI "Dates"
- Talkspace's TalkAI
- Noom's AI food logging

## Q3. Cost structure: human-delivered price points vs AI-native price points

### Takeaway
Human-delivered offerings sit at roughly $150–$800 a month:
- Future: $149–199/month.
- BetterHelp: about $205/month in implied realized revenue per user (list price about $65–100/week).
- WW/Noom: GLP-1 programs from $119/month.

AI-native equivalents price at $0–20/month:
- Ash: free.
- AI coach apps: about $7–13/month.
- Rizz: $19.99/month.
- Cal AI-style trackers: about $30–70/year.

That is a **10–40x price gap**. The gap is largest in therapy and 1:1 training, where human labor is most of the cost. Incumbents are already using AI to stretch human labor (Noom: one coach to 300–400 users; Future's Assistant Coach AI; Headspace and Calm-type AI companions) rather than remove it.

## Q4. Barriers: regulation, liability, platform competition

### Takeaway
AI therapy faces the hardest walls.

**State law**
- Illinois (in force August 2025) bans AI from providing therapy or making therapeutic decisions; fines are reported at up to $10K.
- Nevada (June 2025) bans AI presented as a mental-health professional.
- Utah requires disclosure that the user is talking to AI.

**FDA**
- No generative-AI mental-health device has been authorized.
- The November 2025 advisory committee laid out a risk-based framework: adjunct vs standalone use, prescription vs over-the-counter, post-market monitoring, and change-control plans.

**Market precedent**
- Woebot exited the consumer market in June 2025, citing the pace of AI against the regulatory path.

**Fitness, nutrition, astrology and dating**
- Light regulation; the barriers are retention, distribution and incumbents bundling AI.
- Apple has scaled back its AI health-coach plan (Project Mulberry / "Health+") as of February 2026. That lowers the near-term risk of Apple taking the coaching layer, though camera-based food analysis may still arrive in the Health app.

### Cited Findings

**State laws**
- **Illinois HB 1806 (Wellness and Oversight for Psychological Resources Act)**, signed and effective 2025-08-04:
  - Bans the use of AI to provide therapy or make therapeutic decisions, to generate treatment plans without licensed review, and to detect emotions or mental states.
  - Allows AI for administrative and supplementary support to licensed professionals.
  - Exempts religious counseling, peer support and self-help materials that do not claim to be therapy.
  - Fines reported at up to $10,000. — [Becker's Hospital Review](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/illinois-bans-ai-therapy-without-clinician-oversight/); [Taft Law](https://www.taftlaw.com/news-events/law-bulletins/new-illinois-law-restricts-ai-use-in-therapy-sessions/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/ai/illinois-is-the-first-state-to-ban-ai-therapists-145755797.html)
- **Ash blocked Illinois users**, citing pending policy decisions. — reported in Illinois-law coverage, e.g. [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/illinois-bans-ai-from-providing-therapy-2000639042) (the originating outlet was not pinned down)
- **Nevada (June 2025):** bans programming AI to act as a mental-health professional. **Utah:** requires AI therapy chatbots to disclose that they are AI. — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/ai/illinois-is-the-first-state-to-ban-ai-therapists-145755797.html); [PopSci](https://www.popsci.com/health/ai-therapy-mental-health/)
- AEI notes that enforcement against services that "inadvertently" provide therapy remains an open question. — [AEI CTSE](https://ctse.aei.org/illinois-bans-ai-therapy-questions-about-enforcement-remain/)

**FDA**
- **FDA Digital Health Advisory Committee, 2025-11-06**, on generative-AI digital mental-health devices:
  - More than 1,200 AI-enabled devices have been authorized; **none use generative AI for mental-health conditions**.
  - Risk depends on adjunct vs standalone use, clinician supervision, and prescription vs over-the-counter availability.
  - Topics included post-market monitoring, labeling, human escalation and predetermined change-control plans (PCCPs).
  - Docket FDA-2025-N-2338 (comment deadline given as December 1 or December 8, 2025; sources conflict). — [Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/insights/2025/11/fdas-digital-health-advisory-committee-considers-generative-ai-therapy-chatbots-for-depression); [Psychiatric Times](https://www.psychiatrictimes.com/view/fda-committee-meets-on-generative-ai-digital-mental-health-devices); [Hogan Lovells](https://hlc.com/en/publications/fdas-digital-health-advisory-committee-weighs-guardrails-for-generative-ai-in-mental-health-devices); [FDA meeting materials](https://www.fda.gov/media/190450/download)

**Market precedent**
- **Woebot** closed its consumer app on 2025-06-30. The founder's framing: AI is moving faster than regulators. — [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2025/07/02/woebot-mental-health-chatbot-shuts-down-founder-says-ai-moving-faster-than-regulators/); [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/woebot-health-shutting-down-its-app)
  - Secondary sites attribute the closure to the cost of FDA authorization and the lack of LLM guidance. Not confirmed from STAT's full text.

**Apple**
- **Project Mulberry / Health+:**
  - Bloomberg (March 2025) reported an AI coach that would analyze iPhone, Watch and third-party data, targeted for iOS 19.4.
  - In February 2026 Bloomberg reported Apple had **significantly scaled back** the standalone AI health-coach service after a health-leadership shakeup; Eddy Cue reportedly judged it less competitive.
  - Selected features, including camera-based analysis, will be folded into the Health app over time. — [Gigazine (Bloomberg)](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260206-apple-project-muberry-scaled-back); [iClarified (Gurman)](https://www.iclarified.com/96870/apple-to-revamp-health-app-with-aipowered-doctor-gurman); [TechSpot 2025](https://www.techspot.com/news/107351-apple-new-health-app-feature-ai-doctors-food.html)

**Incumbents bundling AI**

**Retention**
- AI apps churn faster: annual retention 21.1% vs 30.7%. — [RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)
- Health & Fitness has the weakest first renewal (30.3%). — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions/)

### Inferences
- The regulatory line is **"therapy" versus "wellness / self-help / coaching / companion"**. Illinois explicitly exempts self-help materials that do not claim to be therapy. AI-native mental-health apps in the US will most likely position as wellness companions (Finch-like, Ebb-like) or as clinician-supervised adjuncts sold through payers (TalkAI-like), not as standalone "AI therapists". This caps the direct "replace BetterHelp" opportunity at $10–20/month.
- Apple pulling back on Health+ leaves room in 2026 for third-party AI health coaches built on HealthKit data. The risk remains that Apple ships individual features (camera food logging) that commoditize single-feature apps like Cal AI.

## Q5. Per-subcategory verdict: where AI-native, cheaper alternatives can win

### Takeaway
AI has **already won the interface** in calorie tracking (Cal AI; MFP bought it) and is being **absorbed by incumbents** in fitness (Peloton IQ, Strava/Runna), dating (Bumble, Hinge) and meditation (Ebb). The biggest price gaps are in **therapy and 1:1 coaching** (10–40x), but therapy is the most regulated (Illinois, Nevada, Utah, FDA). The most AI-replaceable *human labor* in lightly regulated categories is:
- astrologer and psychic chats
- dating "matchmaker / wingman" advice
- human-written training plans
- narrated meditation and faith content

These markets are smaller (astrology), trust-bound (faith), or protected by network effects (dating).

### Inferences
- **Where AI-native can win.**
  - Clearest pattern: categories where (a) the human labor is advice or text production (plans, readings, coaching messages), (b) regulation is light (fitness, astrology, self-help), and (c) incumbents charge a large human-labor premium (Future at $149–199/month vs AI at $7–13/month).
  - The 1:1 AI fitness coach and the AI astrology/spiritual reader fit best.
  - The AI companion for self-care fits the Illinois "self-help" exemption.
- **Where incumbents absorb AI.**
  - Categories with network effects (dating) or proprietary data and distribution (MFP's food database, Strava's social graph, Peloton's hardware base) are where incumbents absorb AI and AI-native entrants exit by acquisition (Cal AI → MFP, Runna → Strava).
- **Where the price gap is biggest but blocked.**
  - Therapy: incumbents' direct-to-consumer revenue is already shrinking (BetterHelp, Talkspace consumer). But US law and FDA posture push AI therapy toward a non-therapy framing or clinician-supervised B2B2C.
  - Woebot's exit is the cautionary example.
