Which layer comes from which source (one source per layer, never mixed feature by feature):
* central.json roads, rails, water, zones, props: OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors) and some TomTom road segments, both ODbL-1.0, distributed by Overture Maps Foundation.
* central.json buildings and landmarks (footprints, base and top heights, storey counts): Lands Department Building data via the CSDI Portal (CSDI-Terms; not part of the ODbL database).
* central.json building parts (stacked setbacks, crowns and roof structures of 1227 buildings): Lands Department 3D Visualisation Map
  (Non-textured models) via 3d.map.gov.hk and the CSDI Portal (CSDI-Terms; not part of the ODbL database).
* terrain.bin (ground elevation): Civil Engineering and Development Department 2020 LiDAR DTM via the CSDI Portal (CSDI-Terms; not part of the ODbL database).

Changes made: features re-projected to HK1980 Grid (EPSG:2326) in local decimetres; footprints simplified
(0.3 m) and slivers under 4 m² removed; missing building heights estimated; all building and business
names removed; open-sided structures omitted; 6 buildings replaced by fictional landmark placements;
3D building models reduced to their roof surfaces, converted to simplified stacked outlines (0.5 m, up to 4 m for curved outlines) and
fitted to the 2D footprints (no meshes, textures or names kept; none inside sensitive zones or landmarks);
props inside sensitive (military / government) zones removed; terrain resampled to a 5 m grid, outliers
and small gaps repaired, with the sea set to 0 mPD.
